## Privacy Policy for Wingman

**Last updated:** September 09, 2025

Thank you for using Wingman ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we handle your information. Your privacy is critically important to us, and our app is built with a "privacy-first" approach.

### 1. The Core Principle: Your Data is Yours

Wingman is an **offline-first application**. This means that all the data you enter into the app—including contacts, notes, reminders, and settings—is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have servers, and we do not collect, store, view, or transmit any of your personal information. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it using the backup feature.

***

### 2. Information You Provide

We store information that you manually enter into the application. This includes:

* **Contact Information:** Names, birthdays, notes, reminder preferences, last contacted dates, and any custom fields you create (e.g., email, phone number, address). **Wingman does not access your device's system contacts.** All contact data is added by you.
* **Reminder & History Data:** Information about scheduled, completed, snoozed, or dismissed reminders.
* **Application Settings:** Preferences such as default reminder times, notification settings, and onboarding status.

***

### 3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve the application's features on your device. This includes:

* Storing and displaying your contacts and notes.
* Calculating when reminders are due based on your settings.
* Scheduling and displaying notifications for your reminders.
* Showing your interaction history.

All processing and calculations happen locally on your device.

***

### 4. Permissions Required

Wingman requires a few permissions to function correctly. Here’s why we need them:

* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: To show you the reminders you've set up. On Android 13 and higher, this permission is required to display any notification.
* `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`: To schedule your reminders with precision using the system's `AlarmManager`. This ensures your notifications arrive at the correct time, even if the device is in a low-power (doze) mode.
* `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: To re-schedule all your active reminders if you restart your device. This ensures your reminders are not lost after a reboot.

***

### 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We **do not share your personal data with any third parties**. The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data trackers that would transmit your information off your device.

***

### 6. Data Backup and Restore

Wingman offers a manual, user-initiated backup and restore feature.

* **Backup:** When you create a backup, the app generates a single JSON file containing all your app data. You choose where to save this file. You are responsible for the security and storage of your backup file.
* **Restore:** When you restore from a backup, you manually select the file from your device's storage. This process will overwrite any existing data within the Wingman app.

At no point during the backup or restore process is your data sent to us or any third party.

***

### 7. Children’s Privacy

This application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

***

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
