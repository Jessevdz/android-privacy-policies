# Privacy Policy for debestsellers.be

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026

### Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the Facebook application debestsellers.be (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed and maintained by dot.product ("we," "us," or "our"). 

The App is a single-purpose automation tool designed exclusively to publish updates about an aggregated book bestseller list (such as rank changes, new joiners, and risers) directly to a specific, designated Facebook Page. 

**Our core privacy principle is simple: We do not collect, process, or store any personal data. Ever.**

This document explains how the App interacts with Facebook and clarifies the total absence of data collection.

---

### 1. Information We Collect (None)
We are committed to absolute data minimization. **The App does not collect, capture, transmit, or store any personal data from you, your Page followers, or any Facebook users.**

Specifically, the App **does not** collect:
* Names, email addresses, or contact information.
* User IDs, profile pictures, or demographic data.
* Information about individuals who like, comment on, or share the Page's posts.
* Analytics, tracking cookies, or browsing behavior.

---

### 2. How the App Works and Facebook Permissions
To perform its sole function—publishing book list updates to a specific Facebook Page—the App requires certain permissions via the Facebook Graph API. 

The App only requests the minimum permissions necessary to publish content. These typically include:
* `pages_manage_posts` (or equivalent current Facebook API permissions): To allow the App to publish text and link updates regarding the aggregated bestseller list to the connected Facebook Page.
* `pages_read_engagement` / `pages_show_list`: To allow the App to identify the correct Page to publish to.

**How we use these permissions:**
The App uses these permissions strictly in a "write-only" capacity. It generates automated posts about book rankings on our servers and pushes those posts *to* Facebook. The App does not pull, read, or analyze any user data *from* Facebook. The authorization tokens required to do this are used securely and are not tied to the collection of personal user data.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Retention
Because we do not collect any personal data, **we do not store any personal data**. 
* We do not maintain user databases.
* We do not keep logs of Facebook users who interact with the Page.
* The App operates entirely statelessly regarding Facebook user data. 

---

### 4. Third-Party Sharing
We have no data to share, sell, rent, or trade. Consequently, no information is ever shared with third-party advertisers, marketers, or analytics providers.

---

### 5. Data Deletion Instructions
Under Facebook's platform rules, developers must provide users with a way to request the deletion of their data. Because debestsellers.be does not save any of your personal data, there is no data on our servers to delete. 

However, if you are the Page administrator and wish to disconnect the App and revoke its posting permissions, you can do so at any time directly through Facebook:
1. Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy.
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **Security and Login** > **Business Integrations** (or "Apps and Websites").
3. Find debestsellers.be in the list.
4. Click **Remove**.

Once removed, the App will instantly lose the ability to publish to your Page. 

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. 

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in Facebook's API requirements or legal regulations. Because we do not collect email addresses, we cannot notify you individually of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically. The "Effective Date" at the top of this policy will reflect the most recent updates.
