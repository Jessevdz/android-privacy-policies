# Privacy Policy for Cornerstone

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2025
  
## The Short Version: We Collect Nothing

Cornerstone **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data**. All information you create within the app—your meditation history, your journal entries, and your settings—is stored exclusively and securely on your own device. We have no access to it. Period.

To prove this, the Cornerstone application **does not include the `INTERNET` permission**. This means the app is physically incapable of sending any of your data to us or to any third party.

---

## What Information the App Handles

To provide its features, Cornerstone stores the following information locally on your device:

* **Meditation Session History:** The date, time, and duration of each meditation session you complete or log manually. This is used to populate your "History" screen.
* **Journal Entries:** The text you write in your journal and the date it's associated with. This data is for your personal reflection.
* **Timer Settings:** Your preferences for session duration, preparation time, and chosen sounds are saved on your device so you don't have to set them every time.

This data is stored in a private, sandboxed location on your device's internal storage that other apps cannot access.

---

## Data Ownership and Control

You have complete ownership and control over your data. Cornerstone includes features that allow you to manage your information directly:

* **Export Data:** You can export your entire meditation history and all journal entries into a single, human-readable JSON file. You choose where to save this file on your device. This allows you to create backups or move your data to another service.
* **Import Data:** You can restore your practice history from a previously exported backup file. Please be aware that importing a file will **overwrite** all existing data in the app.

These processes are initiated entirely by you and happen only on your device.

---

## Application Permissions

Cornerstone requests a few permissions to function correctly. Here’s what they are and why we need them:

* **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`:** To show a persistent notification while the timer is running. This is crucial for keeping the timer active and accurate in the background and allows you to see the remaining time without opening the app.
* **`FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`:** To run the timer reliably as a foreground service. This prevents the operating system from stopping your session unexpectedly when you switch to another app or turn off the screen.
* **`WAKE_LOCK`:** To ensure your device's processor remains active during a meditation session, preventing the timer from pausing or becoming inaccurate if the device goes into a deep sleep mode.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Cornerstone does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone. As the app collects no data, it is safe for use by all ages.
